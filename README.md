# WorkSlip

**Every job. Every cost. Every payment. Under control.**

WorkSlip is a mobile-first digital job book for independent tradespeople and small workshops. It helps workers manage jobs, customer commitments, expenses, payments, outstanding balances, and profit in one simple workspace.

Built for the NoahAI Nitro 04 Open Build hackathon.

## The problem

Many independent tradespeople manage jobs and payments through memory, paper notes, and chat messages. It becomes easy to lose track of material costs, partial payments, due dates, and what a customer still owes.

## The solution

WorkSlip turns each job into a clear record: what was agreed, what was spent, what has been paid, what remains, and what needs attention next.

## MVP features

- Job creation, editing, search, filtering, and status tracking
- Customer records and job history
- Itemized job expenses
- Partial and full payment tracking
- Live outstanding-balance and profit calculations
- Customer-facing printable receipts and work orders
- WhatsApp share text and CSV/JSON backup tools
- Mobile-first layout with local browser persistence

## Privacy-first by default

The MVP is designed to store data locally in the user's browser. It does not require an account, paid API, or backend. Users should export a backup before clearing browser data or switching devices.
